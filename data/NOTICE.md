# Third-party material

The files in `data/specs/` are third-party documents (manufacturer spec sheets and screenshots of official spec pages), copyright their respective owners. They are included only so the AgentCart hackathon project can be evaluated. They are **not** covered by this repository's MIT licence.

Each product's original source is listed in `data/catalogue.json` as `spec_source_url`. Product and brand names belong to their owners and are used only to identify the products.

Prices in `data/catalogue.json` were checked on the `price_date` shown for each product, at the `price_source_url` shown. They are not live.
