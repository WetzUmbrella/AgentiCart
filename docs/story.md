# The real story (owner: D)

Three sentences, first name only, written consent kept outside the repo. If no real story is found, use a clearly labelled hypothetical and never invent a named person.

>
A person wants to build their dream desk setup. They know exactly what they want it to look and feel like, but they don't know enough about the technical specifications to ensure everything works together.

They spend hours browsing different websites, comparing prices, reading reviews, checking dimensions, and trying to understand compatibility.

Eventually, they have everything they need.

Or so they think.

The monitor arrives, but their laptop cannot support its advertised refresh rate. The microphone arrives, but their audio interface cannot provide the phantom power it needs. The keyboard has the wrong connection. The speakers require cables they never knew existed.

They have spent hundreds, perhaps thousands, of dollars, yet their dream setup is sitting on their desk, incomplete.

The problem isn't that they couldn't find the products.

The problem is that nobody helped them find the right combination of products.
